# T0009 - US label legibility and minimum-size guardrails

## Status
Local implementation complete; not merged or deployed

## Objective
Keep every source-backed OSHA/GHS and DOT field on the CE SilaCure SC label while making the dense-layout GHS pictograms, UN identification number, DOT reference diamond, and supplemental NFPA 704 symbol clearly readable at actual print size.

## Scope
- Preserve all SDS-derived signal word, pictograms, hazard statements, precautionary statements, supplier details, emergency phone, and Section 14 transport fields.
- Increase dense-layout GHS pictograms without omitting or truncating required statements.
- Size the UN/NA/ID marking for the 12 mm requirement that applies to packages over 30 kg / 30 L.
- Keep the approved exterior DOT sticker sheet at 135 mm per side.
- Enlarge the supplemental NFPA 704 footer symbol without displacing required OSHA or DOT content.

## Non-goals
- No independent hazard classification or legal compliance certification.
- No change to the SDS-provided CE SilaCure SC classifications or wording.
- No production deployment without explicit owner approval.
- No claim that the compact footer NFPA symbol is a stand-alone facility placard.
